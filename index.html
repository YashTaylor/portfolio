01. About Me

I'm an Android Developer currently at Adhyay Infotech, where I develop and maintain a suite of three production apps — Mobile Dairy, MD Customer, and MD Tracker — used by over 100,000 people across 10,000+ dairy centers. I take complete ownership of the Android lifecycle: API integration, hardware integrations, testing, deployment, and release management.

Beyond the code, I work directly with clients to understand their needs and collaborate with my manager and product team to shape features. I got my start building scanner-device applications for logistics and port clients like Adani, JSW, and Coromandel, which gave me hands-on experience integrating Android with real-world hardware.

I keep a public Android learning log on GitHub — 18 hands-on mini-projects covering MVVM, Room, Coroutines, Hilt dependency injection, and more. Currently exploring Kotlin Multiplatform and on-device AI with ML Kit to stay ahead of where Android is heading.
